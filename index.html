02Off the clock

From one keyboard to another

When I’m not writing code, I’m usually at the piano, playing or just listening. Bach, Chopin, Debussy and Rachmaninoff are the composers I keep coming back to.

Baroque, Romantic and Impressionist music give us so much room to imagine. Anyone can step into that world as an equal, and it has a quiet way of healing us.

Listening room

Rachmaninoff

Second Piano Concerto in C minor

A few of my favourite moments. Press play and keep browsing; the music carries on in the background.

Public-domain recording from Musopen, via Wikimedia Commons.

  • Baroque

    • Johann Sebastian Bach1685–1750

    Independent voices, each clear on its own, woven into one design. Counterpoint feels a lot like well-built software.

  • Romantic

    • Frédéric Chopin1810–1849
    • Sergei Rachmaninoff1873–1943

    Music that speaks straight from the heart, from Chopin’s intimate nocturnes to the sweeping melodies of Rachmaninoff’s Second Concerto.

  • Impressionism

    • Claude Debussy1862–1918

    Less about telling a story, more about color, light and atmosphere. Clair de lune paints moonlight with sound.
